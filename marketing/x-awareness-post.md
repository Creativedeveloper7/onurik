- Pin the post to the top of the profile for the first weeks.
- Post on a weekday morning East Africa time (8–10am EAT), when Kenyan business accounts are most active.
- Reply to the post yourself with a short thread: one reply per service, each linking to a relevant project on onurik.space/works.
- Reuse the same poster on LinkedIn (16:9 displays in full there too) with option B as the caption.
